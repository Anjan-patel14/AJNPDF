# AJN Brand Search Plan

## Goal

Build a clear, consistent public entity around ANJAN, AJN PDF and AJN Studio so search engines and users can connect the developer, product, studio, guides and original images.

## Implemented

- Consistent brand names in site metadata and structured data.
- Public `/developer` ProfilePage and Person entity with an owned portrait.
- `/ajn-studio` Organization entity connected to the founder and AJN PDF brand.
- WebSite and SoftwareApplication entities for AJN PDF.
- Public `/discover` collection and individual ImageObject pages.
- Main sitemap, image sitemap, RSS feed, canonical URLs and internal navigation.
- Approved public `sameAs` links.
- Descriptive image captions, alt text, rights confirmation and same-domain image delivery.

## Required after deployment

1. Verify the domain in Google Search Console.
2. Submit `/sitemap.xml` and `/image-sitemap.xml`.
3. Inspect `/`, `/developer`, `/ajn-studio`, `/discover` and core tool pages.
4. Keep names, logo, profile image and public social profiles consistent.
5. Publish original useful updates rather than repetitive or keyword-stuffed pages.
6. Earn legitimate mentions and links from relevant communities, product directories and partners.

No implementation can force a first-place result for “AJN”; ranking depends on relevance, authority, competition and search-engine decisions.
