# AJN PDF Monetization First Plan

1. Make the highest-demand tools reliable: Merge, Split, Compress, JPG/PNG to PDF, PDF to image, Protect, Unlock,  and Office conversions.
2. Deploy the FastAPI backend with HTTPS, persistent public-media storage and temporary conversion storage.
3. Publish original guides, screenshots and AJN Discover posts that demonstrate real workflows.
4. Verify Search Console, both sitemaps, Core Web Vitals, legal pages and consent.
5. Confirm AdSense ownership, `ads.txt`, production-only loading and safe placements.
6. Keep ads outside upload, process, progress, result, download, error, admin and legal interfaces.
7. Request AdSense review only when public tools and content are fully usable.
8. Use analytics to improve tools with high error or abandonment rates before adding more ads.

AdSense approval and revenue are controlled by Google and user traffic; they are not guaranteed by the source package.
